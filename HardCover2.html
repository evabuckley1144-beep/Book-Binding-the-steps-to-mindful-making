Hard Cover

Hardcover binding is a structured, multi-step process that transforms loose pages into a durable, lasting object.

The process is divided into stages. Move through them slowly. There is no need to rush.

Overview

Preparing the Text Block

The foundation of the book begins with the pages. Careful preparation ensures the structure will hold together over time.

Sewing the Pages

Individual sections are joined together, forming a unified text block through repetition and precision.

Constructing the Cover

A rigid case is built separately, giving the book its external structure and protection.

Assembly

The text block and cover are joined, completing the form of the book.

Tools & Materials

  • Paper (text pages)
  • Endpaper sheets
  • Needle & thread
  • Ruler
  • Awl or needle for marking holes
  • PVA glue
  • Brush for glue
  • Book cloth
  • Cover boards
  • Cutting knife
  • Bone folder (optional)
  • Weights or press

Step 1

Fold the pages into sections

Take your 4 sheets of paper and fold them into even sections (also called a signature). Press each and fold firmly so the edges align neatly. Repeat this at least 4 times.

Step 2

Mark the spine

Using a ruler or template, lightly mark where stitches will go along the folded edge. Keep spacing even for a stable structure.

Step 3

Prepare your thread

Cut a line of book binding thread (length of the full spine, times the amount of signatures you folded, plus a little extra). Then thread your needle and tie a secure knot at one end.

Step 4

Start the first section

Place your first folded section in front of you. Push the needle through the first marked hole from the inside to the outside.

Step 5

Stitch through each hole

Move through each marked hole in sequence:
- Go out through one hole
- Loop around the edge of the spine
- Come back through the next hole
Keep your movements steady and consistent.

Step 6

Join the next section

When you reach the end of the first section, align the next folded section on top.

Pass the needle through the matching holes and continue stitching the same way as last time, connecting both sections with the same thread.

Step 7

Maintain tension

Pull the thread gently after each stitch. The spine should feel secure, but not too tight or warped.

Step 8

Lock the stitch

At the end of a section, loop the thread around a previous stitch before moving on. This prevents loosening over time.

Step 9

Continue across all sections

Repeat the process until all sections are sewn together into one continuous text block.

Step 10

Finish and tie off

When you reach the final hole, tie a firm knot close to the spine. Trim any excess thread.

Step 11

Repeat

Repeat this process for each signature you have.

Step 12

Reinforce the spine

Use a brush to apply glue along the spine to strengthen the sewn pages. Allow it to dry completely before continuing.

Step 13

Prepare the endpapers

Fold two larger sheets of paper in half. These will become the first and last pages of the book, connecting the text block to the cover.

Step 14

Attach the endpapers

Attach one folded sheet to the front and one to the back of the text block.

Apply glue along the outer fold and press it evenly into place. Let the pages dry flat before moving on.

Step 15

Reinforce the spine

Apply a strip of paper or cloth along the spine.

This reinforces the structure and prepares the book for the next stages. Press it down gently and allow it to dry completely.

Step 16

Build the cover (case)

Measure and cut the boards.

Cut two boards slightly larger than your text block and one the same with and length of your spine. These will form the front and back covers.

Step 17

Space out the pieces

Lay the front board, spine strip, and back board in a row.

Leave small, even gaps between them so the cover can open and close smoothly.

Step 18

Attach the book cloth

Cut a piece of book cloth large enough to wrap around all three pieces.

Apply glue to the cloth, then place the boards onto it in position.

Step 19

Trim

Trim the corners of the cloth.

Press each edge down carefully to create clean, tight corners.

Step 20

Glue the edges

Glue and fold in the edges over the boards.

Press each edge down carefully to create clean, tight corners.

Step 21

Let the cover set

Place the assembled cover under light weight and allow it to dry fully.

Step 22

Attach the text block (gluing your end pages to the cover)

Apply glue to the outer side of the endpapers.

Place the text block into the cover, aligning it carefully before pressing.

Step 23

Press and finish

Close the book and place it under weight.

Allow it to dry completely so the structure sets and the cover bonds securely.

Congratulations, You have come to the end of this journey.

By folding, stitching, and assembling, you have formed loose sheets into a fully realised form.

This is the result of your patience, attention, and focus. We wish you craft again in the future!
