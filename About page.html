About

This website has been created to be an introduction to hard cover book binding. It provides information about the binding process in order to encourage craft as an outlet.

In contrast to today’s hectic and over-stimulating digital environments, this experience is meant to be more reflective and relaxing.

The process of physically binding a book—folding, stitching, and assembling—becomes more than a technique, but a way to return to the tactile and physical world.

Research & Design Approach

These ideas are based on scholarly studies related to craft, mindfulness, and user experience design.

Hands-on creative activities can enhance relaxation and reduce stress. Repetitive, tactile movements such as sewing and folding encourage sustained attention and can lead to a focused, immersive state.

The design of this website is also informed by research on creating calm and accessible digital environments:

Colour

Soft, muted tones and low-contrast palettes help reduce visual strain and create a sense of calm. Neutral backgrounds and subtle accents avoid unnecessary distraction.

Typography

Serif typefaces support readability and encourage a slower pace of reading. Clear hierarchy and minimal styling help guide attention without overwhelming the user.

Layout & Spacing

Generous spacing and a simple column structure reduce cognitive load and allow content to be processed gradually and comfortably.

Interaction

Slow transitions and optional interactions, such as the materials panel, support focus without interrupting the experience.

Environment

Considerations such as lighting, sound, organization, and scent reflect research showing that physical surroundings influence emotional and cognitive states during creative work.
